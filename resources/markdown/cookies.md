This page lists what Dot.Press stores in your browser. It sets only what it needs to work. There are no advertising, tracking or analytics cookies, and no third-party scripts.

## 1. Cookies

| Name | What it is for | How long it lasts |
|---|---|---|
| `dot-press-session` | Keeps you signed in and holds a password unlock for a shared link, if you used one. | Until you close the browser or the session expires after inactivity. |
| `XSRF-TOKEN` | Protects forms and requests from being sent by another site on your behalf. | The same as the session. |
| `remember_web_` followed by letters | Keeps you signed in on this device. Set only if you choose "Keep me signed in on this device". | Until you sign out, or up to five years. |

People who open a shared link receive the session and XSRF cookies too, because the page is served by the same application.

## 2. Local storage

Your browser's local storage on this site holds a few settings and one safeguard. None of it is sent to us by itself.

- `press:theme`: your choice of light or dark.
- `press:ui:v1` and `press:guides:` followed by a deck number: the layout of the editor and the guides you drew.
- `press.presenter.notes` and `press.timer.target`: presenter console settings.
- Changes you made that the server has not yet confirmed, kept so that a closed tab or a lost connection does not lose work. They are removed once the server has them.

## 3. Managing them

The session and XSRF cookies are needed for sign-in and for every form, so the application cannot work without them. You can delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. If you do, you will be signed out and the settings above return to their defaults. Changes that were never confirmed by the server would be lost.
