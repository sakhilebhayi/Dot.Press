Lock-out and isolation: new starter training

Objectives
By the end of this session you will be able to identify hazardous energy sources, apply a personal lock and test that equipment is dead. You will also explain why the procedure exists.

Learning content
Hazardous energy includes electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic and stored mechanical energy. The standard requires every person working on equipment to apply their own lock. The procedure has five steps.
1. Notify the control room and the shift supervisor
2. Switch off and isolate each energy source
3. Apply your personal lock and tag
4. Release or block stored energy
5. Test that the equipment is dead before starting work

Practical application
In the workshop each team isolates the demonstration conveyor, applies locks and completes the permit. The trainer observes each step with a checklist. A short assessment follows, and you must score 80% to be signed off.
