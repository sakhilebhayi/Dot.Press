SolarGrid pitch notes

South African businesses lose an average of 14 days of production a year to load shedding. Small and medium manufacturers cannot afford diesel generators and cannot wait for the grid. This gap is the opportunity: rooftop solar with batteries pays for itself, but financing is complicated and installers are unreliable.

Our market is 38,000 manufacturing sites with a rooftop of more than 500 square metres. We estimate 9,500 of them are ready to buy within three years. The commercial solar market grew 31% last year and no competitor offers a fixed monthly fee with a performance guarantee.

Our strategy is to install and finance systems in one package. We own the panels, the customer pays a fixed monthly fee, and we guarantee output. We will start in Gauteng, then move to the Western Cape and KwaZulu-Natal. The pilot with three factories is complete and all three renewed.

Financials: we expect revenue of R12 million in the first year and R41 million in the third year. Gross margin should reach 38% once we buy panels directly from the manufacturer. We are raising R25 million to fund the first 60 installations.
