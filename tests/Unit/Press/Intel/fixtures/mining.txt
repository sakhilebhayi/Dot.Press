Q3 Production Summary

Run of mine production for the quarter reached 1.2 million tonnes, up 8% on Q2. The plant processed 1.15 million tonnes at an average recovery of 91.4%. Production was strongest in September, when the north pit delivered its best month of the year.

Quarterly production (kt)
Quarter	Tonnes
Q1	1050
Q2	1110
Q3	1200

Safety remains our first priority. We recorded two lost time injuries in the quarter, compared with five in Q2. The lost time injury frequency rate fell to 0.8. However, a conveyor failure in August caused 36 hours of unplanned downtime and cost about 14,000 tonnes of production.

Operating cost per tonne was R412, which is 3% below budget. Diesel prices and a weaker rand remain the main risks to unit costs for Q4. The mill relining project is on track and will be completed by November.

We recommend approving the conveyor replacement programme in Q4. The programme should be funded from the sustaining capital budget. Management should also review the maintenance schedule for the north pit fleet.
