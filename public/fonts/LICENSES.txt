redistribution, including inside a presentation file, as long as the fonts are
not sold on their own and the reserved font names are not used for modified

served by Google Fonts.

fonts.css holds the @font-face rules (font-display: swap). manifest.json lists
each file with its family, style, weight range and subset; the offline present
package reads it to inline only the families a deck uses.
